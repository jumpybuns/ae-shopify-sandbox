# Keyword rules for product tags (used by scripts/syncProducts.mjs and the importer).
# Format:  Tag: keyword, keyword, ...
# A product gets the tag when any keyword appears as a whole word, in any case,
# in its title or description. This is a STARTER LIST: edit it to your taxonomy.
# Tags are only ever added, never removed from a product.

Delay: delay, echo
Reverb: reverb, reverberation
Overdrive: overdrive, overdriven
Distortion: distortion
Fuzz: fuzz
Boost: boost, booster
Chorus: chorus
Phaser: phaser, phase
Flanger: flanger, flange
Tremolo: tremolo
Wah: wah
Limiter: limiter, squash, limit
Compressor: compressor, compression, comp
Looper: looper, loop, drum machine, rhythm
Octave: octave
Pitch Shifter: pitch shifter, pitch shifting, octave
Noise Gate: noise gate, gate
Preamp: preamp, pre-amp
Amp Simulator: amp sim, amp simulator, cab sim, cabinet simulator, IR loader
Tuner: tuner
Multi-Effects: multi-effects, multi effects, multieffects
Equalizer: equalizer, EQ, graphic
Power Supply: power supply
